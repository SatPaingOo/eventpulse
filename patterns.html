Track record

When a severe event happens, EventPulse publishes a call — for example "WTI up at least 2% within 14 days". When the window closes, a fixed rule compares the call to the actual price move and marks it hit or miss. This page is that scoreboard: how often we were right, broken down by event type, direction and asset. No AI is involved in scoring — the market itself is the judge.

1 · call opened→ 2 · window runs (7/14 days)→ 3 · auto-scored at expiry→ 4 · hit / miss recorded

One call travels through both lists: while its window is running it sits in Calls in progress; the day it expires the system compares it with the real close and it moves to Scored calls below — same call, same reasoning, now with a verdict.
