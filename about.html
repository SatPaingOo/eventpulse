How EventPulse works

EventPulse is an experiment in explainable market intelligence: it watches real-world events, connects them to the prices of gold, oil and major companies, and keeps an honest score of its own predictions. Nothing here is financial advice — it's a public, self-auditing record of whether "event X moves market Y" actually holds up.

The pipeline

1

Watch the world

Every few hours we scan global news — politics, conflict, natural disasters, economics — and pull price data for 39 markets across metals, energy and major companies. An AI turns each story into a structured record: what type of event, how severe (1–5), where, and a factual summary.

2

Connect events to prices

When a market moves sharply, we look back at what happened around that time and write why — e.g. "sanctions on Iranian oil → crude +3.4%, gold +2.7% as a hedge". Each explanation is stored with the events it cites, so you always see the chain: this event → this move → this reason.

3

Make calls, then score them

From severe events we publish structured predictions — asset, direction (up/down/range), how much (±%), and a deadline (7 or 14 days). When a call's window closes we compare it to the actual price (no AI in the scoring) and mark it hit or miss. The track record shows, honestly, where we're right and where we're not.

Data & honesty. All records (events, price snapshots, explanations, predictions, scores) are stored as open JSON in the project's data store. Predictions are scored by a deterministic rule against real closes — no model grades its own homework. Explanations are AI-generated hypotheses and are labelled as such; a move with no clear cause is honestly reported as "no candidate events".
